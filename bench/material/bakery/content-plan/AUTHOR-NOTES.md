# Notes from the author

The plan has 30 posts for Crumb & Co., a small bakery that sells sourdough loaves, cinnamon buns and a weekly Loaf Box.
The main goal is Loaf Box pre-orders, so every post points to the pre-order page or to a direct message with the word LOAF.

What I checked: the prices against the price list, the number of reels (12 of 30), and that the pre-order link is the same in every post.
What I did not check: whether the mill claim in post 20 is true, and whether the permissions for customer photos exist.
Three slide designs are offered on the page. Plus any other ideas the panel suggests.
